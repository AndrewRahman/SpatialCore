## The schedule rule (RESEARCH Pattern 3, simulated on this design)

The sender holds the time its next slot is due. The first call after `connect()` sends and arms the
schedule at `now + 1/30`. A later call sends when `now` has reached the due time (compare with a 1e-6 s
tolerance so callers at exact multiples land on the slot), then advances the due time by exactly 1/30;
if `now` is still a full interval or more past the advanced due time (the caller stalled), it resyncs
to `now + 1/30` instead of catching up. Simulated rates over 10 s with +-2 ms jitter: 120 Hz 30.0/s,
60 Hz 30.0/s, 50 Hz 30.0/s, 30 Hz 29.9/s. The naive "now minus last send >= 1/30" rule gives 21 to 23/s
at 60 Hz and must not be used.
