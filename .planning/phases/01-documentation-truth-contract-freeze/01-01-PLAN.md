which documents to update (D-12).

case set, and the first corrected Tier A doc line.

and later changing it breaks consumers who wrote loops or switches against it. **No
`checkpoint:decision` is inserted**, because the user settled 23/15 on 2026-08-11 and
re-asking would be the "gating a decision already made" anti-pattern
(`references/planner-reversibility.md`). The rating is recorded on Task 1 for the record.

consumer inherits — and is flagged on Task 2, not gated.
