<objective>
Answer ADM-OSC position queries the way the standard expects (D-08a): a device that joins late sends
an address with no arguments and gets the current position back. The receiver reports the query to
its Listener, the consumer supplies the position (no store in the receiver, DR-16), and the sender
replies on its next 30 Hz slot to its configured destination (D-20). In the same edit, harden
`ADMOSCReceiver` against wrong-typed, non-finite and out-of-range values (D-21) and silence the five
`-Wunused-parameter` warnings its header raises in every OSD translation unit (D-15).

Purpose: EXTR-04's late-joiner half (D-08a) and the input-safety of the unauthenticated UDP surface
(RESEARCH Security Domain). Today `ADMOSCReceiver` ignores messages with no arguments, calls
`getInt32()` on unchecked arguments, forwards NaN and 1e30 unchanged, and a 1e10 azimuth stored as a
trajectory origin hangs `wrapAzimuth()`.

Output: the query hook and reply path, the hardened receiver, the D-15 check script, and tests.
</objective>
