<objective>
Finish BUG-02 (`Spatial-Media-Lab/OpenSpatialDelay#234`) on SpatialCore's side. The overlap-add the
in-code comment credits with fixing it is already correct (2e-7 against a direct convolution); the
remaining small-block artifact is that the convolver's IR warm-up and crossfade are counted in
*calls*, so at 32-sample blocks a new HRIR is faded in after 160 samples while the IRs are 128-558
samples long. A moving source therefore steps 4.6x harder at 32 than at 512. Make both timings
sample-based with a floor, keep behaviour at 512 and above unchanged for IRs that fit a block, and
add the engine-level tests D-12 sets as the bar. Also remove the KEMAR audio-thread allocation the
dedicated renderer tests meet (CONTEXT D-16 note: the KEMAR resize "is fixed in Phase 3").

Purpose: criterion 2 (no artifacts at 32, 64, 128) and the convolver half of the profile-switch
work (Plan 03-09 applies the same rule to the engine's renderer crossfade).

Output: a sample-timed convolver, a scratch-sized renderer, and five new test cases in the two
dedicated files Plan 03-01 created.
</objective>
