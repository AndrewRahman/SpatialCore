<objective>
Make every profile switch click-free while audio runs (criterion 4), including switches to and from
Simple (D-15). Two causes, both measured: the engine's renderer crossfade is counted in blocks (8 x 32
samples is a 5 ms fade, so a 32-sample switch steps 8x), and a switch to or from Simple is a hard
change of render path. Make the renderer crossfade sample-based like the convolver's (Plan 03-03), and
behind a new default-off `RenderBlockContext::engineSelectsHRTF` flag let the engine choose the path
itself and blend the Woodworth and HRTF outputs during a Simple <-> HRTF fade.

Purpose: criterion 4 and D-15; also the engine half of BUG-02's small-block rule.

Output: the sample-based renderer crossfade, the opt-in flag with dual-path blending, and the click
and dropout tests at all four block sizes.
</objective>

## Ground rules for this plan

- **The user is not a programmer.** Claude runs everything; nothing is asked.
- **Build trees and commands** as in Plan 03-01. Do not edit `tests/CMakeLists.txt` or
  `tests/Binaural/BinauralMetrics.h`.
- **One plan at a time** (Plan 03-01 ground rules): this plan runs after Plan 03-08 (libmysofa pin)
  has committed and before Plan 03-10, never alongside another plan.
- **Measured (research):** profile swap KEMAR → SADIE worst-step ratio 8.19 / 3.23 / 1.15 / 1.00 at
  32 / 64 / 128 / 512 today, 1.00 at all four with a sample-based renderer crossfade prototype
  (`max (8 x block, 4096)`); Simple → KEMAR with the consumer's `useHRTF = ! isSimpleMode()` steps
  15-20x; KEMAR → Simple dips the RMS to about 0.5x. The dual-path fade was specified in research,
  not prototyped (MEDIUM confidence).
- **Why sine for steps and pink noise for dips (planner analysis):** two different renderings of one
  pure tone can partly cancel mid-fade when their phases differ (the HRTF path delays by up to 1-2 ms;
  Simple does not), which would read as a dip on a sine without being a click or a dropout of program
  material. The step criterion uses the sine (where steps are meaningful) and the dropout criterion
  uses broadband pink noise (where it is meaningful). The sine's smallest RMS ratio is printed with
  `WARN` and recorded in the SUMMARY.
- **Switch timing in tests:** call `setHRTFProfile`, `waitForHRTFProfileIdle`, then keep rendering;
  the claim happens at the next block. Detect the switch sample as the first block after which
  `isRendererCrossfadeActive()` is true (or `getHRTFProfileStatus().activeProfile` changes). Leave
  16384 samples before and 12288 after the transition for `switchStepRatio` / `switchMinRmsRatio`.
